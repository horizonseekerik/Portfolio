Core Research — JANUS Architecture

JANUS is my flagship architectural research project (Patent Application No.: 202611052791) — a constraint-aware 3D heterogeneous system demonstrating how spatial waveguide permutation can collapse wide-word matrix multiplication into bounded optical routing. The system integrates:

  • One-Hot Optical Residue Number System (RNS): Replaces analog amplitude estimation (requiring 138.4 dB SNR) with binary spatial localization (SNR > 0 dB) across 256 waveguides per modulus channel.
  • High-Power 1064 nm Passive Fan-Out & TIA-Less APD Readout: 100 W CW Ytterbium-doped fiber laser across a 1 : 2¹⁸ MMI splitting tree delivering +4.61 dB link margin with Ge/Si SAC²M APDs directly driving StrongARM latches (100 aJ/op), eliminating milliwatt TIA amplifiers and ASE noise.
  • Non-Volatile Sb₂Se₃ Beneš Switching: 15-stage dilated Beneš permutation fabric with 3D vertical reservoir Antimony Selenide (Sb₂Se₃) switch cells featuring near-zero optical absorption loss and 0 W static hold power (saving > 100 kW over thermo-optic MZIs).
  • Dual-Cluster PRNS 64-Bit Arithmetic: Polynomial and Parallel RNS diagonalizes 64-bit multiplications into two parallel 8-tile optical streams, achieving sustained exact INT64 throughput of 2,785.3 TMAC/s at 14.91 TMAC/s/W.
  • 3D Z-Axis Thermal Decoupling: Sub-millimeter stack (0.58–0.64 mm, HBM co-packagable) providing a 17× thermal safety margin against optical phase drift.

Foundational Principle: Physical Stability > Theoretical Peak Density. By encoding operand residues as spatial waveguide paths rather than optical amplitudes, JANUS decouples arithmetic precision from analog signal degradation, ensuring deterministic exact computation.

OptiBranch — Quantum-Limited Optical Frontier

OptiBranch investigates parametric amplification physics, phase-coherence, and high-dimensional phase slot encoding:

  • Phase-sensitive parametric amplification (PSA) in Gallium Phosphide (GaP) without excess noise figures.
  • Thin-film lithium tantalate/niobate (TFLN) ultra-fast electro-optic Pockels phase modulation.
  • 10-phase slot encoding for higher-dimensional optical residue mapping.
  • Closed-loop ΔΣ thermal tracking and active PID drift compensation.
