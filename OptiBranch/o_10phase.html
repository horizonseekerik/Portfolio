10-Phase Slot Encoding

OptiBranch extends JANUS's one-hot RNS encoding to 10-phase multi-dimensional slots. Instead of spatial waveguide indices (8 lanes), information is encoded in relative phase differences across temporal slots: each RNS digit m is represented as phase offset Φ_m = (2πm/10) at the output of a TFLN modulation stack.

This approach trades spatial bandwidth for phase resolution, enabling higher information density and immunity to amplitude noise. Detection uses homodyne interferometry with the squeezed state from the PSA stage, achieving shot-noise-limited discrimination across 10 equidistant phases.

Advantages
  • No amplitude noise coupling: Phase encoding immune to ~100× amplitude fluctuations
  • Quantum-limited discrimination: Homodyne detection breaks shot-noise floor by −6 dB (PSA)
  • Scalable: 10 phases fits 2-digit RNS (k ≤ 5) in single modulation stage
