TFLN Pockels Physics

Thin-Film Lithium Niobate (TFLN) exhibits the Pockels effect—a linear electro-optic response to applied voltage: Δn = −½n³r₃₃E, where r₃₃ ≈ 30 pm/V is the nonlinear coefficient. Unlike GaP's χ⁽²⁾, TFLN's χ⁽¹⁾ response is deterministic, broadband, and easily integrated with silicon photonics.

Applied voltage modulates the refractive index, creating a phase shift: Φ = (2π/λ) × n × L. At V_π ≈ 6 V (half-wave voltage), the modulator switches between constructive and destructive interference, enabling high-speed (40 GHz bandwidth) phase modulation.
