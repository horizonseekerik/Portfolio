FWM / XPM / TPA Mitigation

High-power modulation across three WDM lanes triggers third-order nonlinear effects: Four-Wave Mixing (FWM) creates ghost tones at ω₁ + ω₂ − ω₃; Cross-Phase Modulation (XPM) couples phase noise across channels; Two-Photon Absorption (TPA) generates free carriers and nonlinear loss. OptiBranch mitigates these through three mechanisms:

Design Constraints
  • Orthogonal polarization: Lane 0 (TE), Lane 1 (TM), Lane 2 (TE+45°) eliminates XPM
  • Dispersion engineering: D = −5 ps/nm/mm waveguide disrupts FWM phase matching
  • Power budget: Input power < 0 dBm/lane keeps TPA absorption < 0.5 dB over 1 cm
