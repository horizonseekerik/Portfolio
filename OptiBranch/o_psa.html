GaP PSA Stage & Phase Coherence

Gallium Phosphide exhibits the highest nonlinear coefficient among transparent materials: d₃₃ ≈ 150 pm/V. The Parametric Signal Amplifier (PSA) implements quadrature squeezing by converting 1550 nm pump photons (ω_p = 775 nm × 2) into signal-idler pairs via χ⁽²⁾ three-wave mixing.

By measuring both quadratures (amplitude and phase) and conditional on the idler, shot-noise-limited operation is broken: achieving −6 dB quantum noise reduction with no electrical amplification whatsoever. This eliminates thermal noise sources that plague conventional amplifiers.
