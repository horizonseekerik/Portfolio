Thermal Drift & PID Locking

TFLN's Pockels coefficient r₃₃ exhibits strong temperature dependence: dr₃₃/dT ≈ −0.08 pm/V/K. Over a 1K temperature swing, phase modulation can drift by ~25 mrad—sufficient to corrupt phase-slot demodulation. OptiBranch uses an integrated thermistor and resistive heater with digital PID feedback to lock the chip temperature to within ± 0.1 K over environmental swings of 0–40 °C.

The PID controller measures modulator response using a dedicated pilot tone at 5 MHz (inband but orthogonal to data), adjusts heater current accordingly, and achieves steady-state error < 1 mrad within 50 ms after power-on.
