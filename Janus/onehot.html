One-Hot Optical RNS

Bypassing Analog Amplitude Collapse via 256-Waveguide Spatial Localization

Conventional optical neural networks accumulate continuous analog optical power, requiring high-resolution analog-to-digital converters (ADCs). For an unreduced 128×128 INT8 matrix accumulation (spanning S_max = 128 × 255 × 255 = 8,323,200 discrete levels), standard quantization theory demands a minimum SNR of 138.4 dB (23-bit ADC resolution at 100 GHz). Under shot noise, thermal noise, and multi-GHz bandwidth limits, this analog recovery is physically impossible.

JANUS resolves this contradiction by abandoning optical amplitude representation entirely. Instead of encoding numbers into light intensity, JANUS encodes values as a spatial waveguide position using One-Hot Optical Residue Number System (RNS) routing. Photodetection no longer estimates analog magnitude; it answers a binary question: "Does waveguide k contain light?"

Optical Routing Interpretation of Multiplication

Within JANUS, multiplication by a known weight w_i modulo m_i defines a fixed bijective spatial permutation π_w on the residue elements:

Residue x_i ∈ [0, m_i − 1] ⊆ [0, 255]
Waveguide Injection: x_i → e_x (One-Hot Select)
Modular Multiplication: π_w : x_i ↦ (x_i · w_i) mod m_i
Binary Output: D_k = 1 if light present in channel k, else 0

Optically, a modular multiplication is realized without arithmetic computation: light entering input waveguide x_i is routed deterministically through the non-volatile Beneš switch fabric to exit at output waveguide z_i = (x_i · w_i) mod m_i. Optical energy is conserved spatially (Σ P_i = P_total), eliminating analog loss from weighted attenuation.

Active Electro-Optic Injection (LiTaO₃ Pockels)

To avoid broadcast-and-attenuate loss, JANUS employs a 1 × 256 active electro-optic micro-ring router at each multiplier input fabricated in Lithium Tantalate (LiTaO₃). Leveraging the linear Pockels effect rather than carrier injection, the router achieves:

  • Ultra-Low Dynamic Energy: ≈ 50 aJ per switch transition at 100 GHz.
  • Datacenter Router Power: Total power across 134 million active routers is bounded to P_router = 134×10⁶ × 50 aJ × 100 GHz ≈ 8.19 W.
  • Single-Carrier Coherence: Light is injected exclusively into the active One-Hot channel; dark channels receive zero power, preserving carrier phase coherence across all branches (Δϕ_source ≈ 0).
