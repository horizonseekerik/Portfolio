One-Hot Optical RNS Encoding

Conventional RNS processors sample optical intensity — requiring SNR > 138 dB for 8-bit precision. JANUS sidesteps this entirely: each residue value is encoded as a spatial waveguide index, not a signal amplitude.

A residue of value v selects exactly waveguide v (out of 256). The detector only asks "is there light or not?" — a binary presence test. This collapses the SNR requirement from 138 dB down to > 0 dB, making the system immune to optical noise at the arithmetic level.
