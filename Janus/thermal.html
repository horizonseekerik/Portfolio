Thermal Controller

GST-467 phase-change cells are highly sensitive to temperature. Even a few degrees of drift can shift the amorphous-to-crystalline transition point, causing routing errors. The JIR Thermal Controller monitors all photonic tile thermal diodes using a ΔΣ ADC and runs a real-time slope estimator to predict thermal runaway before it occurs.

When a tile's temperature slope exceeds the safety threshold, the controller predictively migrates active computations to redundant tiles without stalling the pipeline — analogous to TI TMP117 / Analog Devices AD7124 firmware.
