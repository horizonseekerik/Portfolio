Fabrication & Yield Analysis

JANUS uses a heterogeneous 3D Z-stack: a silicon photonics (SiPh) die bonded face-to-face onto a CMOS logic die using imec's copper-to-copper (Cu-Cu) die-to-die interconnect at ~250,000 vias/mm². LioniX thermo-optic heaters control waveguide phase, and Ligentec provides the ultra-low-loss Si₃N₄ waveguide platform.
