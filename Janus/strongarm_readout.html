StrongARM Dynamic Readout

100 aJ Event-Driven Sense Amplification & Interleaved Two-Layer Ge/Si APD Arrays

Scaling to 134,217,728 physical photodetectors within a single 300 mm² Datacenter die presents an insurmountable static power barrier if implemented using conventional continuous-time transimpedance amplifiers (TIAs). Linear TIAs consume ~50 mW per active channel, demanding 30 kW of static electrical power and exceeding chip thermal extraction limits by two orders of magnitude.

JANUS eliminates continuous analog linearity in favor of event-driven dynamic StrongARM regenerative latches integrated intimately with the Ge/Si SAC²M APD array. Because JANUS operates on a strictly sparse One-Hot basis (where only 1 in 256 waveguides carries light per multiplier, spatial activity α = 1/256), dynamic power is consumed exclusively during photon-triggered state transitions.

Interleaved Two-Layer Detector Block Layout

To route 134.2 million photodetectors without metal interconnect congestion, JANUS organizes detectors into an interleaved two-layer detector block:

  • Dual-Subarray Stacking: Two vertically stacked Ge/Si SAC²M APD sub-arrays handle alternating odd and even waveguide channels, addressed through separate metal layers.
  • Wiring Pitch Optimization: Halves the required metal pitch per wiring layer, suppressing capacitive inter-detector electrical crosstalk.
  • Spatial Thermal Uniformity: Distributes localized detector power evenly across the 201.32 mm² detector plane directly beneath Heat Spreader 1 (HS1).
Complete TIA Elimination & Direct APD-to-Latch Readout

Conventional optical receivers require multi-stage analog Transimpedance Amplifiers (TIAs) to convert photocurrent into voltage. TIAs consume milliwatt-scale continuous power (~50 mW/ch), suffer from high Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise, and introduce severe analog bandwidth bottlenecks. JANUS completely removes the TIA stage.

Instead, the Ge/Si SAC²M Avalanche Photodetector (APD) operates in an unbuffered direct-coupling mode: the avalanche displacement current generated by an incoming optical pulse (3–5 ps FWHM) directly charges/discharges the sub-femtofarad input gate capacitance (C_in ≤ 3 fF) of a clocked StrongARM dynamic regenerative latch. On the rising clock edge (100 GHz wave-pipelined phase), the cross-coupled inverter pair triggers rapid positive feedback, regenerating a full-rail digital CMOS logic level (0 V / 0.9 V) in < 5 ps.

Detector Operating Point & Noise Immunity

Operating at an optimal avalanche gain M = 7 with low McIntyre excess noise factor F(M) ≈ 2.0, the SAC²M APD achieves high optical sensitivity without excess multiplication noise. For a target bit error rate of 10⁻¹⁸ (Q ≈ 9.38, < 1 undetected error per century), practical receiver sensitivity is P_sens ≈ −23.20 dBm (4.79 µW), delivering a robust +4.61 dB binary margin above the noise floor without requiring optical pre-amplifiers or ASE noise.
