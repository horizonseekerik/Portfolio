CMOS Digital Backend

100 GHz Wave-Pipelined Digital Reconstruction, StrongARM Readout, and Hybrid Partitioning

Many all-optical accelerator proposals attempt to eliminate electronic logic entirely. However, exact arithmetic verification, CRT integer reconstruction, memory coherence, and tile orchestration are fundamentally digital tasks that electronics executes with deterministic perfection. JANUS intentionally adopts a hybrid architectural philosophy:

Photonics → Massively Parallel Passive Transport & Waveguide Routing
CMOS Logic → Deterministic Verification, Control & Exact Integer Reconstruction

This division allows each physical substrate to operate within its native strengths. Photonics delivers massive bandwidth density with zero hold power, while the 50 µm CMOS base die provides deterministic arithmetic verification at a 100 GHz clock rate (10.0 ps cycle period).

Event-Driven Dynamic Latched Readout

Scaling to 134,217,728 physical photodetectors in the Datacenter configuration requires extreme mitigation of static power. Conventional continuous transimpedance amplifiers (TIAs) consume ~50 mW per channel, which would draw 30 kW of static power and destroy the chip thermally.

JANUS eliminates TIAs in favor of event-driven dynamic StrongARM regenerative latches. Operating on a sparse One-Hot basis (where only 1 in 256 waveguides carries light, spatial activity α = 1/256), hierarchical wake-up gating ensures inactive latches remain asleep. Combined with ultra-low input capacitance (C_int ≤ 3 fF) and column-shared S/H multiplexing, the electrical energy cost is reduced to ≈ 100 aJ (0.1 fJ) per active decision, bounding total Datacenter detector dynamic power to just 5.24 W.

Wave-Pipelined Digital Integration
  • Single-Cycle Ingestion: Input operands are partitioned and converted into One-Hot spatial commands in a single 10.0 ps cycle slot.
  • Adder-Tree Latency Hiding: The 210 ps CMOS CRT reconstruction latency is deeply pipelined into 21 sub-stages of 10.0 ps each, executing in parallel with optical transit.
  • Full CMOS Power Breakdown: Total CMOS power under sustained 100 GHz full-load operation is 2.01 W (Mini), 8.50 W (Edge), and 37.03 W (Datacenter).
