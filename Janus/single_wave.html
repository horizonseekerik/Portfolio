Single-Wavelength Injection

Nonlinear Optical Suppression & 50 aJ LiTaO₃ Electro-Optic Pockels Routers

Many optical neural accelerators attempt to scale computational density through Dense Wavelength-Division Multiplexing (DWDM), where multiple optical carriers propagate simultaneously through shared waveguides. However, as optical intensity increases, Kerr nonlinearities (n = n₀ + n₂I) generate severe inter-channel interference—including Four-Wave Mixing (FWM), Cross-Phase Modulation (XPM), and Spectral Beating Noise—that scale superlinearly (P_NL ∝ γ·P³·L²) and corrupt analog signals before photodetection.

JANUS abandons multi-wavelength scaling entirely. Instead, the architecture operates on a single optical carrier frequency (N_λ = 1 at 1064 nm), scaling throughput purely in the spatial domain (T ∝ N_spatial). Without multiple carrier frequencies, conditions for inter-channel Four-Wave Mixing collapse (ω₁ = ω₂ = ω₃ ⇒ ω_FWM = ω), structurally suppressing ghost frequencies (P_ghost → 0) and inter-channel entropy transfer (E_i→j → 0).

Active Electro-Optic Injection (LiTaO₃ Pockels Routers)

Because JANUS uses a single optical carrier, the optical power can be injected efficiently into the computation mesh without passive broadcast-and-attenuate losses. Each multiplier input incorporates a 1 × 256 active micro-ring router fabricated in Lithium Tantalate (LiTaO₃):

  • Linear Pockels Electro-Optic Effect: Exploit capacitive field-effect switching rather than thermal phase-tuning or free-carrier plasma dispersion, eliminating carrier recombination latency and thermal drift.
  • Sub-Femtojoule Switching: Capacitive charge and full cycle discharge yields an ultra-low dynamic switching energy of ≈ 50 aJ per transition at 100 GHz.
  • Datacenter Router Power: Total electrical power across all 134,217,728 active input routers is bounded to P_router = 134×10⁶ × 50 aJ × 100 GHz ≈ 8.19 W.
  • Spatial Entropy Isolation: Only the designated One-Hot waveguide receives optical energy (dark channels receive zero light), preserving carrier link margin and isolating computational entropy across channels (∂C_i / ∂C_j ≈ 0).
