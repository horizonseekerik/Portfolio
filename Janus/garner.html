CRT Garner Pipeline

The Garner Pipeline is a 4-stage fixed-function arithmetic unit that reconstructs the full 64-bit integer from k residues in exactly 5 clock cycles (152 ps at 33 GHz). This represents a 30% improvement over the naïve sequential CRT approach (210 ps).
