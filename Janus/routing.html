Beneš Routing & PCM Switching

The optical crossbar is a dilated non-volatile Beneš network built on Ge₂Sb₂Se₄Te₁ (GST-467) phase-change cells over Si₃N₄ waveguides. Each cell toggles between amorphous (transparent) and crystalline (reflective) states on nanosecond timescales without holding power — a fully non-volatile routing state.

Mechanical reliability is achieved through a vertical reservoir design: a sacrificial GST migration sink sits atop each active cell, absorbing elemental drift across 10⁶–10⁸ switching cycles. Void-gap isolation rings prevent delamination stress from propagating across adjacent waveguide crossings.
