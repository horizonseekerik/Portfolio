Predictive Clock Gating

At INT4 precision (k=1 modulus), 31 of 32 compute tiles are idle. Without clock gating this idle circuitry dissipates near-full dynamic power. JANUS's Predictive Clock Gating Network pre-gates power domains during weight-loading, eliminating switching activity in idle tiles before the computation window opens.

At k=1, 96% of pipeline dynamic power is saved. This is architecturally equivalent to ARM DynamIQ's per-core power domain isolation and Apple Neural Engine's weight-load pre-gating.
