Power & Throughput Profiles

Multi-Precision Scaling (INT4 to INT64), Subsystem Power Budgets, and Energy Efficiency Metrics

A central breakthrough of the JANUS architecture is sustaining exact, bounded integer precision across arbitrary precision targets (INT4 through INT64) without sacrificing throughput density or energy efficiency. Because modular residue multiplication scales logarithmically through parallel One-Hot channels, computational throughput scales directly with active tile parallelism.
