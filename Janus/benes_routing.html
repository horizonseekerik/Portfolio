Dilated Beneš Routing

15-Stage Non-Volatile Permutation Fabrics with 3D Vertical Reservoir Switch Cells

Conventional optical matrix accelerators rely on thermo-optic Mach–Zehnder Interferometer (MZI) meshes that consume continuous electrical bias power (~0.10 mW per switch). At datacenter scale, a thermo-optic mesh would demand 100.67 kW of continuous static heat dissipation directly on the chip, inducing severe thermal crosstalk and continuous recalibration drift.

JANUS eliminates static hold power entirely by replacing thermo-optic heaters with non-volatile Antimony Selenide (Sb₂Se₃) phase-change material (PCM) switches arranged in a 15-stage dilated Beneš network topology. Unlike conventional GST alloys that suffer from high optical absorption in the crystalline state, Sb₂Se₃ exhibits near-zero optical absorption loss (Δk ≈ 0 at 1064 nm) with high refractive index contrast (Δn ≈ 1.2–1.5), allowing pure phase modulation without excessive attenuation. The optical state is latched non-volatilely after programming (0 W static hold power), drawing energy strictly during weight reconfigurations (~10–50 pJ per switch).

15-Stage Dilated Beneš Topology
  • Universal 15-Stage Depth: Because One-Hot RNS operates on a 256-waveguide basis, each per-multiplier routing fabric requires exactly S = 2·log₂(256) − 1 = 15 stages, regardless of global matrix dimension.
  • Logarithmic Switch Complexity: Each per-multiplier Beneš fabric contains N_switch = (256/2)·(15) = 1,920 switches, scaling as O(N log N) compared to O(N²) for full crossbars.
  • Full Fabric Deployment: The Datacenter tier deploys 524,288 Beneš fabrics across 32 tiles, containing 1,006,632,960 (1.006 Billion) non-volatile Sb₂Se₃ switches operating at 0 W static power.
  • Dilated Crosstalk Suppression: Active and dummy paths are physically disjoint (Active ∩ Dummy = ∅). Simultaneously routed channels never share switching elements, structurally suppressing coherent crosstalk leakage.
  • Predictable Low Optical Loss: Total routing loss is bounded to L_routing = 15 stages × 0.5 dB = 7.5 dB, fitting comfortably within the 1064 nm passive laser power budget.
3D Vertical Reservoir Switch Geometry (Sb₂Se₃)

Conventional PCM switches degrade over repeated melt-quench cycles due to liquid-phase segregation. JANUS optimizes the Sb₂Se₃ phase-change switch through engineered vertical reservoir architecture:

  • Near-Zero Absorption (Δk ≈ 0): Sb₂Se₃ provides transparent optical phase shifting in both amorphous and crystalline states at 1064 nm, slashing insertion loss per switch cell to < 0.5 dB.
  • Nanoscale Void Gap (~25 nm): The PCM cell is split into an upper thermal reservoir (V_reservoir) and a lower optical interaction layer (V_optical) separated by an engineered air void.
  • Thermal Isolation: Air thermal conductivity (k_air ≈ 0.026 W/m·K) is 54× lower than SiO₂, keeping the optical datapath cool during write cycles.
  • Mechanical Buffer: Accommodates the volumetric transition between phases, preventing delamination across 10⁶–10⁸ operational write cycles.
