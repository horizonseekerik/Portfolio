Passive Fan-Out & APD Array

High-Power 1064 nm Carrier Distribution, Noise-Free Passive Splitting, and +4.61 dB Link Closure

Conventional optical accelerators propose embedding intermediate optical amplifiers (such as SOAs) within the optical datapath. However, semiconductor optical amplifiers consume over 30 kW of electrical bias power across a large-scale mesh and introduce fatal Amplified Spontaneous Emission (ASE) noise (σ²_ASE ∝ G·n_sp·B) that irreversibly degrades signal fidelity. Similarly, microring parametric amplifiers encounter a fundamental Quality Factor versus Bandwidth Wall (Q ≤ 3,200 for 100 GHz pulses), failing under energy conservation bounds.

JANUS eliminates in-line optical amplification entirely. Instead, a master high-power 1064 nm continuous-wave Ytterbium-doped fiber laser (100 W / +50.0 dBm in Datacenter, WPE > 75%) passively distributes carrier light across a 1 : 2¹⁸ optical splitting tree to terminal high-speed Ge/Si SAC²M avalanche photodetectors (APDs), achieving link closure with a robust +4.61 dB binary detection margin and zero ASE noise.

1 : 2¹⁸ Passive Distribution Budget & Link Margin

The optical carrier is distributed across 18 cascaded 1 : 2 multimode interference (MMI) splitters (N = 262,144 output ports):

Ideal 18-Stage Splitting Loss:   L_split = 10·log₁₀(2¹⁸) ≈ 54.19 dB
Excess Insertion & Path Loss:    L_excess = 5.40 dB (MMI) + 7.50 dB (15-stage Beneš) + 1.50 dB (coupling) = 14.40 dB
Total End-to-End Loss:           L_total = 54.19 dB + 14.40 dB = 68.59 dB

Delivered Terminal Power:        P_det = +50.0 dBm − 68.59 dB = −18.59 dBm (13.84 µW)
Receiver Sensitivity (BER 10⁻¹⁸): P_sens,practical ≈ −23.20 dBm (4.79 µW)
Net Binary Detection Margin:     Margin = (−18.59 dBm) − (−23.20 dBm) = +4.61 dB (2.88× Power Safety Factor)
Ge/Si SAC²M Avalanche Photodetector Physics

Terminal detection uses Separate-Absorption-Charge-Cliff-Multiplication (SAC²M) Ge/Si avalanche photodetectors:

  • Low Excess Noise: Confining impact ionization to a pure silicon cliff layer achieves an ionization ratio of k ≈ 0.06. At gain M = 7, the McIntyre excess noise factor is F(M) ≈ 2.0 (far below conventional InGaAs APDs where F > 5).
  • Ultra-High Bandwidth: Gain–bandwidth product of 441 GHz with an inherent 3 dB receiver bandwidth of 105 GHz, comfortably resolving 100 GHz optical pulses.
  • Fast Carrier Clearance: Photogenerated carriers clear the multiplication cliff in t_PD = 1 / (2π · 105 GHz) ≈ 1.52 ps, enabling complete reset well within the 10.0 ps repetition slot.
  • TIA-Less Direct Coupling: The SAC²M APD directly drives the sub-femtofarad input gate of a clocked StrongARM regenerative latch, completely removing continuous-time transimpedance amplifiers and saving > 30 kW of static receiver power across the array.
